# Evidence and safety rules

- Preferred sources guide search; they are not automatic endorsements.
- Live search records the canonical URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval time, and supporting excerpt.
- Repeated coverage of one development is grouped before the Editor selects items.
- The Editor may explain a possible business implication but may not predict a price or recommend a trade.
- Unfamiliar finance language is defined briefly in place.
- Thin or conflicting evidence is labeled; confidence is not manufactured.
- The UI shows observable actions and structured outputs, never private chain-of-thought.
- Demo fixture copy is visibly identified as synthetic.
