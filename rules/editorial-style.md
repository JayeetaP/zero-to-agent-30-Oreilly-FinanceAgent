# Editorial style

- Lead with the conclusion or market implication.
- Use short paragraphs, active voice, and direct verbs.
- Define unfamiliar finance terms in place.
- Use human-readable dates and currency formats.
- Do not use em dashes.
- Avoid stock phrases such as "delve into," "navigate the landscape," "underscores the importance,"
  "in today's rapidly evolving world," and "not just X, but Y."
- Do not repeat labels when a section heading already explains the content.
- Do not claim certainty that the evidence does not support.
- Do not predict prices or recommend trades.
